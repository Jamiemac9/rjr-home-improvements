## What you're getting

A website built to bring in roofing work around Bromsgrove, Rubery and Rednal, with emergency repairs first.

- **A quote form on every page**, plus its own Contact page. Customers pick from dropdowns (what they need, how urgent, their area, property type, how and when to reply), add their name, postcode and what's happening, then press **Continue to WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens on their phone with the whole message written out to 07542 373810; they add photos and press send. Every enquiry arrives with the same details, in the same order.
- **The form is already filled in for the page they're on.** On the Rubery storm damage page, for example, "Storm damage repairs", "Rubery" and "Emergency" are pre-selected.
- **A Call button** on every page, and a Get a quote / Call bar fixed to the bottom of the screen on phones.
- **Emergency pages** for leaks, storm damage and urgent repairs, with a simple "what to do right now" guide for customers.
- **A page for each service:** emergency repairs, storm damage, roof leaks, roof repairs and re-roofing, chimneys, flat roofs, guttering with fascias and soffits, and leadwork, plus brickwork and building work. Each covers what goes wrong, how the job is done, what affects the price, and common questions.
- **A page for each area**, written around the houses there, the council that handles planning, and local conditions such as wind on the Lickey and Clent Hills or the 1960s–80s estates in Redditch. Each area also has pages for your six main roofing services.
- **Your before & after photos**, each set checked to be the same job.
- **Your reviews**, word for word, with links to your Google Business Profile and Rated People.
- **Cookie banner, privacy policy and cookie policy.**
- **Fast on phones.** Pages are small, and photos are resized so phones only download what they need.
