## What's built

- **Lead offer:** emergency roof repairs, storm damage, roof leaks.
- **Main action:** the quote form at the end of every page, and on its own at `/contact/`.
  - Dropdowns: service, urgency, area, property type, reply method, best time. Text fields: name, postcode, details.
  - "Continue to WhatsApp" opens wa.me/447542373810 with a structured message.
  - Each page pre-selects its own service and area, and urgency "Emergency" on emergency pages.
  - Nothing is sent to or stored by the site.
- **Pages:** home, contact, services index, 12 roofing service pages (in-depth: causes, options, process, cost factors, prevention, FAQs), 3 grouped trade pages, areas index, 10 area pages, 60 area × service pages, before & after, reviews, about, privacy, cookie, sitemap.
- **Areas:** Edgbaston, Kings Heath, Moseley, Solihull, Shirley, Hall Green, Harborne, Selly Oak, Stirchley, Northfield.
- **Local content system:** each area has housing `features`. Area × service pages are built from `FEATURE_NOTES[service][feature]` Q&As, so advice differs by housing. Wording shared between area pages averages about 45%.
- **Proof:** 6 verified photo jobs (`PROJECTS`) and 5 verbatim Rated People reviews. "Excellent", 127 ratings. Linked to the Google Business Profile.
- **Header:** sticky; shortens on scroll; menu button on small screens (Cursor, PR #1, code in `consent.js`).
- **SEO/AEO:** answer-first intros, JSON-LD (RoofingContractor with company number, Service, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, ContactPage), split sitemaps, `llms.txt`, unique titles under 70 chars.
- **Legal:** cookie consent (Accept/Reject equal weight; analytics only after Accept; none installed), privacy policy, cookie policy.
- **Speed:**
  - Home HTML ~8 KB and CSS ~5 KB, both compressed.
  - Two small scripts.
  - Responsive WebP images; pre-blurred hero images ~20 KB.
  - Non-blocking Google Fonts.
- **Demo mode:** `DEMO=1` gives noindex + robots `Disallow: /`. `serve.py` uses it by default.
