- **Area × service pages (78):** the 6 "matrix" services × 13 areas. Built from `FEATURE_NOTES[service][feature]`, with at least 3 local Q&As per page. Wording shared between area pages averages about 42%.
- **Quote form** on every page and `/contact/`:
  - Dropdowns: service, urgency, area (13 + "Elsewhere within 20 miles of Bromsgrove"), property type, reply method, best time.
  - "Continue to WhatsApp" opens a pre-filled message to 447542373810.
  - Each page pre-selects its own service and area, plus Emergency on emergency pages.
  - Nothing is sent to or stored by the site.
- **Photos:** 9 verified jobs in `PROJECTS`: extension re-roof, chimney rebuild, felt flat roof, gable fascias, lead flashing, Velux extension, grey-tile re-roof, canopy strip, lean-to porch. Plus the van photo.
- **Schema:** RoofingContractor with a 20-mile GeoCircle round Bromsgrove (`SITE["geo"]`), named area Places, Service, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, ContactPage. Geo meta tags use Bromsgrove (`GB-WOR`).
- **Hosting files:**
  - `static/_redirects`: old Birmingham area URLs and retired services → nearest new page (301).
  - `static/_headers`: caching, security headers, and the CSP that blocks Netlify's injected badge.
